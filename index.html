Our Mission

We turn superfans into professionals.

Formula 1 hires thousands of people who never touch a steering wheel — engineers, strategists, designers, marketers, logisticians. Our mission is to hand the next generation a clear, free, project-based route into every one of those jobs.

01 / Problem

Fragmented Resources

Industry paths are scattered across forums, dead links and gatekept PDFs. We centralize the map so you always know your next corner.

02 / Method

Project-Based Learning

No boring lectures. Learn by designing liveries, analyzing telemetry and writing real marketing pitches — work that ships to a portfolio.

03 / Source

Industry Led

Learn directly from interviews and masterclasses with real F1 and motorsport professionals — the people already doing the job.
